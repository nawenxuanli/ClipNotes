# ClipNotes Study Guide Privacy Policy

ClipNotes Study Guide for Windows reads the video files you choose and transcribes their audio on your device. Course file paths, transcripts, saved timestamps, and study notes are stored in the app's local data folder. The app does not upload recordings, transcripts, or notes and does not require a ClipNotes account.

The first transcription may download the Whisper speech model from Hugging Face. The model provider receives the network information needed to serve the download. See the [Hugging Face Privacy Policy](https://huggingface.co/privacy) for its practices.

You can control access to the app's local data through your Windows account. To remove saved course information, close the app and delete its local data. This does not delete the original recordings. Exported Markdown files remain wherever you saved them.

For privacy questions, use the [ClipNotes support page](https://github.com/nawenxuanli/ClipNotes/issues). Please do not include recordings, transcripts, or personal information in a public issue.
